minutes of daylight today

Sun hours tells Home Assistant how long the day is at your home, and whether today is longer or shorter than yesterday. In this sample, 2 minutes shorter.

Download sun_hours-1.0.0.hapkg
DawnWhat it does

Two numbers from where you live

It uses the home location already set in Home Assistant to work out the time between sunrise and sunset, in minutes, and compares it with the day before. Nothing is fetched from the internet.

MorningSetup

One step, no questions

  1. Check your home location under Settings, System, General.
  2. Settings, Devices and services, Add integration, Sun hours.
  3. Press Submit. The sensors appear straight away.

AfternoonOptions

How to count, how to show

Choose whether daylight is shown as minutes or as hours and minutes, and whether the day ends at the horizon or at civil twilight. The location always follows your home location.
