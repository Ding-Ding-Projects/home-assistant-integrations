Seed tray, from sown to planted out

Tell Home Assistant what you sowed and when. It follows each sowing through germination to transplant day and counts down the days in between.

What it does

Three stages, worked out from two numbers

Every sowing has a sow date, the days it usually takes to germinate, and the days until it is ready to transplant. From those the integration reports where it is now.

GerminatingGrowing onReady to transplant
Setup

Sow, then note it down

  1. Settings, Devices and services, Add integration, Seed tray.
  2. Pick a crop or type your own name.
  3. Set the sow date, days to germinate and days to transplant.
Entities created

Two sensors per sowing

Options

Adjust as the tray tells you

Seeds do not read the packet. Open Configure to change the days to germinate or transplant for any sowing, move its sow date, or remove it once it is planted out.

Install

Drop the file on the Marketplace

Download the package, open the Marketplace page in Home Assistant, and drag the file anywhere onto it. Check the source and the verified checksums, confirm, then restart when asked. On a phone, use Install from a package file.
